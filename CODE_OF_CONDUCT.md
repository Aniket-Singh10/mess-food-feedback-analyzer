# Code of Conduct

## Our Goal

We want this project to be a welcoming and friendly place for everyone. All contributors are expected to treat each other with respect and professionalism.

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected Behavior

* Be respectful and kind to others.
* Give constructive feedback.
* Be open to different ideas and perspectives.
* Help create a positive environment for learning and collaboration.
* Communicate politely in issues, discussions, and pull requests.

## Unacceptable Behavior

* Harassment, bullying, or discrimination of any kind.
* Offensive, abusive, or insulting language.
* Personal attacks or unnecessary arguments.
* Sharing someone else's private information without permission.
* Any behavior that makes others feel unsafe or unwelcome.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct.

## Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please contact the project maintainers. All reports will be reviewed and handled appropriately.

## Conclusion

By participating in this project, you agree to follow these guidelines and help maintain a positive and inclusive community.
